Privacy Policy

This page explains how information may be collected and used when you interact with the DODS website.

Important: Learning Project DODS is a demonstration e-commerce project created for performance marketing, analytics and tracking practice. This policy is a demo and should be replaced with a legally reviewed privacy policy before using a real commercial website.

1. Introduction

DODS respects your privacy and aims to handle information responsibly. This demo policy describes the general categories of information that may be collected when users browse the website, interact with products, submit forms or place orders.

By using this website, you acknowledge that information may be processed for the purposes described below.

2. Information We May Collect

Information You Provide

Depending on the interaction, users may voluntarily provide information such as:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Delivery address
  • Order information
  • Messages submitted through contact forms

Automatically Collected Information

Website analytics and measurement tools may collect technical and behavioural information such as page views, interactions, device information, browser information, traffic source and approximate usage data.

3. Analytics & Measurement

This demonstration project is designed to work with analytics and marketing measurement tools such as Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager.

These tools may help understand how visitors interact with the website, which pages receive traffic and which marketing activities contribute to business outcomes.

Example Measurement Events

  • Product views
  • Add-to-cart actions
  • Checkout activity
  • Purchase events
  • Contact form submissions
  • Phone and WhatsApp click intent

Personally identifiable information such as a user's name, email address or phone number should not be intentionally sent to Google Analytics event parameters.

4. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Processing and managing orders
  • Providing customer support
  • Responding to enquiries
  • Improving website experience
  • Understanding website performance
  • Measuring marketing campaigns
  • Detecting security or technical issues

5. Cookies & Similar Technologies

Websites may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, measure website usage and support analytics or advertising functionality.

The actual use of cookies depends on the technologies implemented on the production website.

6. Third-Party Services

A real e-commerce website may use third-party services for analytics, payment processing, advertising, hosting, shipping and customer support.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices

Depending on applicable law and the nature of the information, users may have rights relating to their personal information.

  • Request access to certain information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion where applicable
  • Manage certain cookie or browser settings
  • Contact the business with privacy questions

9. Children's Privacy

This demonstration website is not specifically designed to collect information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

A production business may update its privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in services, technology, legal requirements or business practices.

Users should review the latest version of the policy when changes are published.
