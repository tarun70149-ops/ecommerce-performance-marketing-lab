Return Eligibility

To keep the return process simple, returned products should generally meet the following conditions.

  • Return request should be raised within the applicable return window.
  • Product should be unused and in acceptable condition.
  • Original packaging and relevant accessories should be retained.
  • Product should not show signs of intentional damage or misuse.
  • Certain products may have different return eligibility.

Refund Information

When is the refund processed?

Once the returned product has been received and approved, the refund process can begin.

Where will the refund go?

Refunds are generally returned through the applicable original payment method, subject to the payment provider's process.

What if my product is damaged?

Contact support as soon as possible and provide the relevant order details so the issue can be reviewed.
